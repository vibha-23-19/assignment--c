1.Write a short paragraph in your own words defining what a 'program' is and give
 one real-life example from any app you use daily (like Instagram, Zomato, or Paytm).
 
 A program is a set of step-by-step instructions written by a programmer in a language that a
 computer can understand and execute, in order to perform a specific task. These instructions tell 
 the computer exactly what actions to take and in what order. 
 
 A real-life example is Instagram it follows instructions to let users create accounts, upload photos and videos, 
 scroll through a feed, like and comment on posts,and send direct messages to other users.
